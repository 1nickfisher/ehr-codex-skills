# Disclaimer

The skills in this repository are **guidance, not legal, medical, or clinical advice**. They synthesize publicly available regulations, official program guides, and accepted practice into a format that agent-based development tools can apply during software work — but they are not a substitute for:

- A licensed clinician's judgment about care.
- A licensed attorney's interpretation of regulation.
- Your organization's compliance officer's policies and procedures.
- The current published versions of the authorities each skill cites.

**Skills are dated. Regulations change.** When a skill cites a BHIN, CFR section, ONC rule, state policy manual, or accreditation standard, treat the citation as the source of truth — not the skill body. Verify the cited authority is still current before relying on any specific rule.

If a skill is used in a healthcare product, the implementing organization remains responsible for the correctness, completeness, and currency of every compliance check the product performs. The presence of a skill does not transfer that responsibility.

Contributors and maintainers provide this content **"as-is" under the MIT License**, without warranty of any kind, including (without limitation) any warranty that a skill reflects the most recent regulation, accepted clinical practice, or your jurisdiction's specific requirements.

## What to do if you spot something stale

1. Open an issue identifying the skill, the section, and the authority that has changed.
2. Cite the new authority (URL, section number, effective date).
3. If you can, open a PR following the requirements in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

We commit to addressing source-change issues with the same care that produced the original skill — including domain-expert review before any change merges.
